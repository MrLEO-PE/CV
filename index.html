About

What drives me is building a PE environment where every student can find their own motivation, whatever their starting point or their relationship with sport.

I have taught in French and international schools since 2019, adapting lessons across the French, UK and IPC systems, and I specialise in IGCSE PE and the Cambridge curriculum. I have also worked as a PE coordinator, planning the programme and organising school events that bring students and parents into the life of the school.

When existing tools did not fit how I wanted to teach, I built my own. They are web apps, so students and staff open them in a browser on any device with nothing to install: student self-assessment, IGCSE theory and practice, gym training, and programme planning for the PE team.

PE Apps

A set of tools I built for my own teaching, to make assessment, tracking and planning faster and clearer for both students and staff.

Self Assessment App

Students rate their own performance against the five skill areas of the PE model, then compare it with the teacher assessment. It makes progress visible to the student and speeds up marking for the teacher.

Step-by-step guide

Gym Weight Training Tracker (IGCSE)

A training log for IGCSE students in the gym. They record their personal bests on each machine and follow their sessions across endurance, volume and strength goals, so training choices are based on their own data.

Step-by-step guide

Online IGCSE Workbook

An interactive revision workbook covering the whole Cambridge IGCSE PE theory syllabus: 203 exam-style questions marked instantly by AI, labelling diagrams, flashcards and practice exams. Students can see how secure they are on every syllabus point, and teachers can see which topics each class needs to revisit.

Step-by-step guide

Teacher Timetable Planner

A curriculum mapping tool for the PE programme. It lays out the units across year groups and terms and shows the teaching workload for each teacher, so the year is balanced before it starts.
